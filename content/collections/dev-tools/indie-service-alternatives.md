INDIE SERVICE ALTERNATIVES
==========================

MUSIC & DISCOVERY
- [Maroofy](https://maroofy.com/) - Find remixes and musical versions of songs
- [Chosic](https://www.chosic.com/) - Music discovery tools, playlists, and listening stats
- [Every Noise at Once](https://everynoise.com/) - Music genre map and discovery
- [ListenBrainz](https://listenbrainz.org/) - Open-source music listening tracker (like Last.fm but open)
- [MusicScape](https://musicscape.app/) - Visualize your music taste and discover new artists
- [Songs Like X](https://www.songslikex.com/) - Find songs similar to ones you love

READING & BOOKS
- [StoryGraph](https://www.thestorygraph.com/) - Book tracker with mood/content filters (alternative to Goodreads)
- [Literal Club](https://literal.club/) - Social reading app for tracking and discussing books
- [Openlibrary](https://openlibrary.org/) - Free book catalog and lending (open-source alternative)
- [Readng](https://readng.co/) - Minimal book tracking and reading goals
- [Bookbrainz](https://bookbrainz.org/) - Open-source book database
- [AnyList](https://www.anylist.com/) - Track reading lists and share with friends

SUPPORT & MONETIZATION
- [Buy Me a Coffee](https://www.buymeacoffee.com/) - Simpler Ko-Fi alternative with tiered memberships
- [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/) - Creator support with membership tiers
- [Polar.sh](https://polar.sh/) - Developer-first support platform
- [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/) - Creator marketplace and payment processor
- [Ghost](https://ghost.org/) - Membership and paid newsletter platform
- [Substack](https://substack.com/) - Newsletter with paid subscription option

INDIE DATA & PRIVACY-FOCUSED TOOLS
- [Neocities](https://neocities.org/) - Free indie web hosting community
- [Basecamp](https://basecamp.com/) - Project management (indie-friendly alternative to Slack sprawl)
- [Bear Blog](https://bearblog.pro/) - Minimal blogging platform ($3/month)
- [Are.na](https://www.are.na/) - Visual bookmarking and collaboration tool
- [Obsidian Publish](https://obsidian.md/) - Personal knowledge base with publishing option
- [Logseq](https://logseq.com/) - Open-source knowledge management

GAMES & HOBBIES
- [Itch.io](https://itch.io/) - Indie game distribution platform
- [BoardGameArena](https://boardgamearena.com/) - Play board games with friends online
- [Retroachievements](https://retroachievements.org/) - Achievement tracking for classic games

COMMUNITY & NICHE INTERESTS
- [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/) - Movie tracking and social network (like Goodreads for film)
- [Mastodon](https://mastodon.social/) - Open-source social network alternative
- [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/) - Decentralized social media (open protocol)
- [Discord Communities](https://discord.com/) - Still indie-friendly for niche communities

PHOTOGRAPHY & VISUAL
- [Flickr](https://www.flickr.com/) - Photography community (still independent-feeling)
- [Glass](https://glass.photo/) - Photography social network
- [Pixelfed](https://pixelfed.social/) - Open-source Instagram alternative

COMMON THEMES THESE SERVICES SHARE
==================================
✓ Focused on ONE thing well (not everything)
✓ Human-scale interfaces (no algorithm bloat)
✓ Privacy-respecting (no ad tracking)
✓ Community-driven or indie-built
✓ Transparent monetization (subscription or donation, not ads)
✓ Character and personality in the design
✓ Reasonable pricing ($3-10/month range, or free)
