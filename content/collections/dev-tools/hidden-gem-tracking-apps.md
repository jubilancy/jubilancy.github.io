HIDDEN GEMS: NEW & UNKNOWN TRACKING PLATFORMS (2025-2026)
=========================================================

🎵 MUSIC (Unknown/New)
======================

ROVR
- Status: 2025-2026, quietly gaining followers
- What: Anti-algorithm global music curators selecting handpicked tracks daily
- Vibe: Pure human curation, no algorithmic bloat, discovery-focused
- Social: Community of music nerds sharing playlists with reasons why
- Try if: You want real people picking music, not Spotify's algorithm

Indie Shuffle
- Status: Cult classic, still under 1M users despite being incredible
- What: Human-curated indie music playlists, zero algorithm
- Vibe: Feels like having a music-obsessed friend send you playlists
- Social: Minimal but engaged community in comments
- Try if: You love indie/alternative and want to discover before mainstream hits

Audiomack (Underground Mode)
- Status: 2025 redesign, still flying under the radar
- What: Like SoundCloud but with better discovery for unsigned artists
- Vibe: Street-level buzz before major label deals
- Social: Active artist community, comment threads on unreleased tracks
- Try if: You want to find the next Playboi Carti before he's huge

Qobuz (Listening Tab)
- Status: Quiet listening community, not a social network but acts like one
- What: Audiophile streaming with a "listening now" feed you can follow
- Vibe: High-fidelity focus, curated picks, minimal social pressure
- Social: Passive following of what others are listening to in real-time
- Try if: Sound quality matters + you want to see what real audiophiles play

Bandsintown (Live Discovery)
- Status: 2025 refresh, most underused feature by average listeners
- What: Tracks artists you follow and alerts when they play near you
- Vibe: Connects discovery to real-world experiences
- Social: Concert-going community, not followers but real connections
- Try if: You want your music taste connected to local live music


🎬 FILM (New/Unknown)
====================

Achriom (AI Librarian Mode)
- Status: Brand new 2025-2026, nobody's heard of it yet
- What: AI librarian for tracking movies/books/music/anime in ONE place
- Vibe: Ask "what should I watch if I liked Past Lives?" and it reasons through your taste
- Social: None (intentionally private), but AI discovery is wild
- Try if: You want AI that actually understands YOU across all media

TOME (Launched January 28, 2025)
- Status: 6 months old, extremely small community but growing
- What: "Twitter meets WordPress meets Booktok" but for books AND films
- Vibe: Aesthetic profile customization, beautiful review writing, community is tiny and intentional
- Social: Community-first, just added dark mode in Feb 2026, not yet saturated
- Try if: You want to write beautiful reviews in a community that cares about aesthetics

Moviebase (Overlooked Social Mode)
- Status: 2025-2026, excellent tracker that nobody talks about
- What: Beautiful movie tracker with Trakt sync + movie recommendations by mood
- Vibe: Minimal, clean, anti-algorithmic, data-portable
- Social: Statistics and lists, not as social as Letterboxd but way better designed
- Try if: You want tracking + discovery without the Letterboxd influence-chasing

Cinopsys
- Status: Completely free, completely unknown, zero ads
- What: Bare-bones movie tracker that just works
- Vibe: Anti-features, anti-ads, anti-corporate, anti-everything
- Social: None intentionally
- Try if: You want pure utility with zero bloat


📚 BOOKS (New/Unknown)
======================

PageBound (2024-2025 growth phase)
- Status: Growing indie platform, still under 500K users
- What: "What if Goodreads and Reddit had a baby?" — book-specific forums + tracking
- Vibe: Every book has its own discussion room with spoiler tags, designed for readers not influencers
- Social: Community-first but intentional and thoughtful
- Built as: Explicitly anti-AI platform, indie team vocal about values
- Try if: You want REAL book discussions with people actually reading the same book

TOME (2025 launch)
- Status: Same as above — brand new, Instagram-like for books with aesthetics
- What: Beautiful profile design, reviews, reading lists with visual appeal
- Vibe: You can make your reading aesthetic match your vibe (cosplay photos, bookshelf photos, etc)
- Social: New and small, community still forming
- Try if: You're into BookTok aesthetics but want something indie and slow-growing

Fable (Redesigned 2025)
- Status: Acquired by Everand/Scribd in early 2025, redesigned completely
- What: Community-first with book-specific forums, spoiler-safe chapter discussions
- Vibe: "Book clubs" but built into the app, discussions organized by reading percentage
- Social: Healthy community, not oversaturated with influencers yet
- Try if: You like discussion + tracking and don't mind corporate backing if the product is good

Bookology (iOS-only, 2025)
- Status: Brand new, iOS-only, feature-rich but tiny user base
- What: Automatic reading timer, calendar heatmap, detailed reading stats
- Vibe: StoryGraph-level analytics but designed for early adopters
- Social: None
- Try if: You're iOS-only and love data but want to avoid saturation

ReadHero (2025 redesign)
- Status: Note-taking focus, nobody uses this but it's brilliant
- What: Built around writing notes AS YOU READ to improve retention
- Vibe: Academic/thoughtful approach, reading playlists, quote collections
- Social: Reading buddies (minimal social)
- Try if: You read non-fiction and want to actually retain what you learn

READO
- Status: Very new, barely 50K users
- What: Has "Booklyn" AI assistant + Goodreads import + buddy reads
- Vibe: AI-assisted but community-focused, not pushy
- Social: Buddy reads and groups without the influencer pressure
- Try if: You want AI help but in a small, genuinely kind community

Hardcover (Still gaining audience, 2024-2025)
- Status: Growing from small base, not oversaturated
- What: Social network first, tracker second (opposite of Goodreads)
- Vibe: Genuinely new community, 0-100% match scores for recommendations
- Social: Want to read / Currently reading / DNF tracking with real discussions
- Try if: You want the Letterboxd experience but for books in a smaller community

LibRea (Upcoming 2026, very underground)
- Status: In beta, less than 1K users aware of it
- What: Decentralized book tracking with privacy focus
- Vibe: Open-source, data ownership, federated like Mastodon
- Social: Will have social but indie-web style
- Try if: You want to be there at the absolute beginning of something


🎯 PATTERN: WHAT MAKES THESE DIFFERENT
======================================
• Less than 1M users (or niche within platform)
• Intentional community design (not algorithmic growth)
• Either anti-AI or thoughtfully integrated AI
• Aesthetic/design-forward (not utilitarian)
• Small indie teams or cooperative models
• Launched 2024-2026
• Privacy-respecting (data export, no ad tracking)
• Not trying to be the next Spotify/Goodreads/Letterboxd

The sweet spot right now: TOME + PageBound + ROVR
Communities are tiny enough to have character, but established enough to have actual users.
