## 📚 Essential Reading & Monographs

*   **Collins, Patricia Hill. (2004). *Black Sexual Politics: African Americans, Gender, and the New Racism*.**
    *   *Core Theme:* Examines how historical stereotypes of Black sexuality are recycled in modern media and how the community internalizes these scripts.
*   **Higginbotham, Evelyn Brooks. (1993). *Righteous Discontent: The Women's Movement in the Black Baptist Church, 1880–1920*.**
    *   *Core Theme:* The foundational text introducing the concept of the "Politics of Respectability."
*   **Harris-Perry, Melissa V. (2011). *Sister Citizen: Shame, Stereotypes, and Black Women in America*.**
    *   *Core Theme:* Analyzes the damaging impacts of the "Jezebel," "Mammy," and "Angry Black Woman" myths on modern political and psychological realities.
*   **Morgan, Joan. (1999). *When Chickenheads Come Home to Roost: A Hip-Hop Feminist Breaks It Down*.**
    *   *Core Theme:* Delves into the cognitive dissonance of loving hip-hop while navigating its rampant sexism and hypersexuality.

## 📰 Peer-Reviewed Papers & Studies

*   **The Adultification of Black Girls**
    *   *Source:* [Georgetown Law Center on Poverty and Inequality](https://georgetown.edu "Georgetown Law Study")
    *   *Insight:* Data proving that external society views Black girls as younger, less innocent, and more hypersexualized than white peers.
*   **"Strange Bedfellows: Respectability Politics and Black Feminism"**
    *   *Source:* [ResearchGate Publication](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/249880603_Strange_Bedfellows "Strange Bedfellows Paper")
    *   *Insight:* Explores how contemporary Black female entertainers use explicit performances to push back against rigid community standards.
*   **"Examining Black Male Sexual Narratives"**
    *   *Source:* [Duquesne University Scholarship](https://dsc.duq.edu/etd/1461/ "Black Male Sexual Narratives")
    *   *Insight:* Qualitative study on the psychological exhaustion and fragmentation Black men face due to external hypersexual labeling.
