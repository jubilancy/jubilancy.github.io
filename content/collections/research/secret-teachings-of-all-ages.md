_The Secret Teachings of All Ages_ (1928) by Manly P. Hall is a comprehensive encyclopedia, not based on a single source, but rather a synthesis of nearly 600 recognized authorities, ancient texts, and rare manuscripts covering Masonry, Hermeticism, Qabbalistic traditions, and Rosicrucian philosophy. [1](https://www.amazon.com/Secret-Teachings-All-Ages-Encyclopedic/dp/0893145467), [2](https://www.amazon.com/Secret-Teachings-All-Ages-Encyclopedic/dp/1639234225)
Key Sources and ThemesHall drew upon centuries of hidden wisdom and esoteric literature to compile his "magnum opus":
- Hermeticism & Alchemy: Teachings of Thoth Hermes Trismegistus, _Isis the Virgin of the World_, and various alchemical texts.
- Qabbalah (Kabbalah): Explores the Secret Doctrine of Israel, Cabbalistic keys to creation, and the Tree of the Sephiroth.
- Masonic & Rosicrucian Studies: The Hiramic Legend and symbolic philosophy of Rosicrucianism.
- Ancient Mythology & Philosophy: Pythagorean theory of music/color, Atlantean mythology, and Greek, Egyptian, and Nordic mysteries.
- Symbolism & Occultism: Insights into the Tarot, Ceremonial Magic, and the Bembine Table of Isis. [2](https://www.amazon.com/Secret-Teachings-All-Ages-Encyclopedic/dp/1639234225), [5](https://www.prs.org/store/p2838/Vol._1_-_Study_Guide_to_The_Secret_Teachings_of_All_Ages%3A_Based_upon_the_Introduction_to_An_Encyclopedic_Outline_of_Symbolical_Philosophy.html), [6](https://www.cia.gov/library/abbottabad-compound/E4/E4AAFF6DAF6863F459A8B4E52DFB9FF4_Manly.P.Hall_The.Secret.Teachings.of.All.Ages.pdf), [7](https://store.doverpublications.com/products/9780486471433), [8](https://blas.com/the-secret-teachings-of-all-ages/)
Key Contributors & Artistic Source
- J. Augustus Knapp: The book is famous for its 54 stunning full-page color plates, illustrated by this noted artist.
- Research & Archives: Much of the material was curated while Hall was studying rare occult works, often summarized in his "Encyclopedic Outline".
- Study Guide/Companion: A _Study Guide to the Secret Teachings of All Ages_ and _The Seeker’s Guide to the Secret Teachings_ (by Mitch Horowitz) serve as companion sources. [1](https://www.amazon.com/Secret-Teachings-All-Ages-Encyclopedic/dp/0893145467), [4](https://www.amazon.com/Secret-Teachings-All-Ages/dp/1998614360), [5](https://www.prs.org/store/p2838/Vol._1_-_Study_Guide_to_The_Secret_Teachings_of_All_Ages%3A_Based_upon_the_Introduction_to_An_Encyclopedic_Outline_of_Symbolical_Philosophy.html), [9](https://www.amazon.com/Seekers-Guide-Secret-Teachings-Ages/dp/1722503181#:~:text=The%20Seeker's%20Guide%20to%20the%20Secret%20Teachings,Esoteric%20Landmark:%20Horowitz%2C%20Mitch:%209781722503185:%20Amazon.com:%20Books.)
The book was first published in 1928 in an oversized format and has since been re-released by publishers like Dover and the Philosophical Research Society (PRS), which Hall founded.
