## Overview of the Artwork

- Subject: A caricature of a seventeenth-century plague doctor wearing a protective beak mask and heavy garments meant to ward off infectious miasma. [1, 3, 4]
- Text: The caption at the bottom reads _"Kleidung wider den Tod zu Rom. Anno 1656"_ (Attire against death in Rome, 1656), accompanied by satirical German and Latin verses describing the costume and the practices of physicians during pestilence outbreaks. [1, 2]
