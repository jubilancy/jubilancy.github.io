The [Day One Project](https://fas.org/day-one-project/) is *a nonpartisan initiative by the [Federation of American Scientists (FAS)](https://fas.org/expert/day-one-project/) that democratizes science and technology policymaking by helping experts turn innovative ideas into action-ready federal policy memos*. [1](https://fas.org/day-one-project/), [2](https://www.idealist.org/en/nonprofit/67918b16756d40f98b4057040feb377c-federation-of-american-scientistsday-one-project-washington)
Core Mission & Approach
- **Policy Entrepreneurship:** Empowers scientists, technologists, and researchers to design concrete proposals rather than just highlighting societal problems. [1](https://fas.org/day-one-project/)
- **Day One Memos:** Short, actionable policy recommendations built for immediate adoption and adaptation by decision-makers. [1](https://fas.org/day-one-project/)
- **Priority Focus Areas:** Covers key pillars including [Energy and Environment](https://fas.org/day-one-project/energy-and-environment/), R&D and innovation, government capacity, global security, and emerging technologies like artificial intelligence. [1](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/federation-of-american-scientists_day-one-project-federation-of-american-activity-7196545759243104256-SRWk)
How to Participate
- **Open Call:** Submissions are accepted through the [FAS Submission Platform](https://fas.secure-platform.com/), with the current Open Call open through December 31, 2026. [1](https://fas.secure-platform.com/)
- **Accelerators & Sprints:** Writers receive training, feedback, and coaching over a 4-to-12-week program in exchange for participating in structured policy-writing cohorts. [1](https://fas.org/day-one-project/)
- **What You Need to Apply:** A concise idea summary, data or research supporting the plan, the target societal problem, urgency factors, and the intended government decision-maker. [1](https://fas.org/day-one-project/)
