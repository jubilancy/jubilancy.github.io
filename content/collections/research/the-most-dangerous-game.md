## Adaptations

#wiki 

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=The_Most_Dangerous_Game&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Adaptations")\]

Main article: [Adaptations of The Most Dangerous Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adaptations_of_The_Most_Dangerous_Game "Adaptations of The Most Dangerous Game")

The story has been adapted numerous times, most notably as the 1932 [RKO Pictures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RKO_Pictures "RKO Pictures") film *[The Most Dangerous Game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Most_Dangerous_Game_\(1932_film\) "The Most Dangerous Game (1932 film)")*, starring [Joel McCrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joel_McCrea "Joel McCrea"), [Leslie Banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leslie_Banks "Leslie Banks"), and [Fay Wray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fay_Wray "Fay Wray"),<sup about="#mwt32" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-7" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-7&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwZw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwaA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaQ">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwag">]</span></span></a></sup> and for a 1943 episode of the [CBS Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS_Radio "CBS Radio") series *[Suspense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suspense_\(radio_program\) "Suspense (radio program)")*, starring [Orson Welles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orson_Welles "Orson Welles").<sup about="#mwt35" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-8" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-8&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-8" id="mwbw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwcA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwcQ">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwcg">]</span></span></a></sup>
