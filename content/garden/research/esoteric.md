# terms

1. **Abraxas**: A mystical Gnostic symbol representing the unity of opposites and the divine source of creation.
2. **Aether**: In esotericism, the subtle fifth element beyond earth, air, fire, and water; the medium of spirit.
3. **Akasha**: The Sanskrit term for the universal etheric substance; often associated with memory or the “Akashic Records.”
4. **Albedo**: In alchemy, the whitening stage of purification and enlightenment following the dark night of nigredo.
5. **Alkahest**: The legendary universal solvent sought by alchemists, capable of dissolving any substance.
6. **Anima Mundi**: The world soul; a spiritual essence that permeates and unifies all living things.
7. **Anthropos**: The archetypal “divine human” in Gnosticism and mysticism, symbolizing cosmic man.
8. **Apocatastasis**: The esoteric doctrine of ultimate restoration, where all beings return to divine unity.
9. **Archeus**: The vital principle or formative spirit that bridges the material and spiritual worlds.
10. **Asclepius**: A Hermetic text attributed to Hermes Trismegistus; also the Greek god of healing.
11. **Astral Plane**: A non-physical realm of subtle existence, often reached through dreams or meditation.
12. **Atman**: In Hinduism, the eternal self or soul identical with the universal spirit (Brahman).
13. **Augoeides**: The radiant higher self or guiding spirit in Neoplatonic and Hermetic traditions.
14. **Azoth**: The universal medicine and essence in alchemy; symbol of wholeness and transformation.
15. **Bardo**: In Tibetan Buddhism, the intermediate state between death and rebirth.
16. **Binah**: The third Sephirah on the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, representing understanding and the divine feminine.
17. **Bodhisattva**: One who seeks enlightenment but vows to help all beings achieve liberation first.
18. **Brahman**: The absolute, unchanging spiritual reality in Vedantic philosophy.
19. **Caduceus**: The Hermetic staff entwined with serpents, symbolizing balance, healing, and spiritual power.
20. **Chokmah**: The second Sephirah in Kabbalah, embodying wisdom and dynamic creative force.
21. **Daath**: The hidden, non-numbered Sephirah on the Tree of Life; a gateway of knowledge.
22. **Daimon**: A guiding spirit or intermediary between humans and the divine in Greek esotericism.
23. **Demiurge**: In Gnosticism, the creator of the material world, often seen as flawed or ignorant.
24. **Dhyana**: Deep meditation or absorption in Buddhist and yogic practice.
25. **Egregore**: A collective thought-form or psychic entity generated by a group’s shared beliefs.
26. **Ein Sof**: In Kabbalah, the infinite, unknowable source of all creation.
27. **Elixir Vitae**: The elixir of life in alchemy, conferring immortality or spiritual renewal.
28. **Emanation**: The process by which divine reality unfolds into levels of existence.
29. **Ens**: A metaphysical being or essence in alchemical and mystical philosophy.
30. **Eon**: Vast cosmic age; in Gnosticism, divine emanations or beings.
31. **Eschaton**: The ultimate destiny or end of the world in mystical or apocalyptic traditions.
32. **Etheric Body**: A subtle energy body that vitalizes the physical body in occult physiology.
33. **Gnosis**: Direct experiential knowledge of spiritual truth, central to Gnostic traditions.
34. **Hekate**: Goddess of liminality, witchcraft, and magic in ancient and modern esoteric practices.
35. **Hermetica**: Texts attributed to Hermes Trismegistus, foundational to Western esotericism.
36. **Hieros Gamos**: The sacred marriage of opposites, symbolizing union of divine masculine and feminine.
37. **IAO**: A Gnostic and magical name of power, often representing the cycle of creation.
38. **Imago Dei**: The “image of God” within humanity, symbolizing divine likeness.
39. **Initiation**: A transformative ritual or process of spiritual awakening and entry into mysteries.
40. **Ishtar**: Mesopotamian goddess of love, war, and initiation.
41. **Kabbalah**: Jewish mystical tradition exploring divine emanations and the Tree of Life.
42. **Karma**: The law of moral cause and effect across lifetimes in Hindu and Buddhist thought.
43. **Kether**: The crown Sephirah in Kabbalah, representing pure divine unity.
44. **Kundalini**: Latent spiritual energy envisioned as a coiled serpent at the base of the spine.
45. **Lapis Philosophorum**: The Philosopher’s Stone, symbol of perfection, enlightenment, and immortality.
46. **Logos**: The divine Word or rational principle ordering the cosmos.
47. **Lucifuge**: Occult figure associated with hidden knowledge and avoidance of light.
48. **Lumen Naturae**: “Light of nature,” the inner illumination guiding alchemical work.
49. **Magus**: A magician or adept of esoteric wisdom.
50. **Mandala**: Sacred symbolic diagram representing the cosmos, used in meditation.
51. **Mantra**: Sacred sound or phrase repeated to invoke spiritual power.
52. **Maya**: The illusory nature of the phenomenal world in Indian philosophy.
53. **Merkabah**: Mystical Jewish tradition of ascent through visionary chariots and heavens.
54. **Metatron**: Archangel and scribe in Jewish mysticism, associated with divine presence.
55. **Monad**: The ultimate unity or singular source of all existence.
56. **Mysterium Magnum**: The great mystery, ultimate reality in alchemy and mysticism.
57. **Nephesh**: The vital soul or life force in Kabbalah.
58. **Nephilim**: Mythical giants or fallen beings from Hebrew scripture and esotericism.
59. **Nirvana**: Liberation from the cycle of rebirth in Buddhism.
60. **Nous**: The divine intellect or higher mind in Neoplatonic thought.
61. **Occultum**: Hidden spiritual essence or secret center in alchemy.
62. **Orenda**: Spiritual power or energy in Native American traditions.
63. **Orphic Egg**: Symbol of cosmic origin in Orphic and esoteric cosmology.
64. **Ouroboros**: The serpent eating its tail, symbolizing eternal return and unity.
65. **Paraclete**: The Holy Spirit as advocate and guide in Christian mysticism.
66. **Philosopher’s Stone**: The ultimate goal of alchemy, transmutation of base matter to perfection.
67. **Pneuma**: Spiritual breath or soul principle in Greek esoteric philosophy.
68. **Prima Materia**: The raw, undifferentiated substance in alchemy, source of creation.
69. **Prana**: Vital energy or life-force in Indian philosophy.
70. **Qliphoth**: In Kabbalah, the “shells” or husks representing impure forces.
71. **Ruach**: Spirit or breath in Hebrew; the middle soul in Kabbalistic anthropology.
72. **Samadhi**: Union or absorption in yogic meditation, highest state of consciousness.
73. **Satori**: Sudden enlightenment in Zen Buddhism.
74. **Sephiroth**: The ten emanations of divine attributes in Kabbalah.
75. **Shekinah**: The divine feminine presence or indwelling in Kabbalah.
76. **Shunyata**: The Buddhist concept of emptiness, ultimate nature of reality.
77. **Siddhi**: Supernatural powers attained through yogic practice.
78. **Sophia**: Divine wisdom, often personified in Gnostic traditions.
79. **Spiritus Mundi**: The world spirit or collective soul.
80. **Sri Yantra**: A sacred geometric diagram symbolizing cosmic creation and union.
81. **Subtle Body**: The energetic body beyond the physical, used in esoteric physiology.
82. **Tattva**: Fundamental principles or elements in Indian cosmology.
83. **Telesma**: The vital force permeating matter, mentioned in the Emerald Tablet.
84. **Tetragrammaton**: The four-letter name of God (YHWH) in Hebrew mysticism.
85. **Thelema**: Esoteric philosophy of will, systematized by Aleister Crowley.
86. **Therion**: Symbol of the Beast in Thelemic tradition, representing primal force.
87. **Thoth**: Egyptian god of wisdom, writing, and magic; identified with Hermes.
88. **Tiphareth**: The Sephirah of beauty, balance, and harmony in Kabbalah.
89. **Upanishad**: Philosophical scriptures exploring ultimate reality in Hinduism.
90. **Vajra**: Ritual weapon symbolizing indestructibility and enlightenment in Buddhism.
91. **Vril**: A supposed occult energy popularized in 19th-century esoteric fiction.
92. **Yetzirah**: The world of formation in Kabbalistic cosmology.
93. **Zohar**: Foundational mystical text of Kabbalah.

# Christian Esotericism

the inner and/or mystical aspect of the Christian Religion, it includes:

1. **Christian Gnosis (Clement of Alexandria)**: The pursuit of spiritual knowledge within early Christianity, emphasizing inner illumination and synthesis of faith with philosophy.
    
    - _The Stromata_ – Clement of Alexandria
    - _Exhortation to the Greeks_ – Clement of Alexandria
    - _Christian Gnosis_ – Jean Daniélou
2. **Desert Fathers Spirituality (Evagrius Ponticus)**: The mystical teachings of early Christian hermits and monks, focusing on prayer, asceticism, and purity of heart.
    
    - _The Praktikos & Chapters on Prayer_ – Evagrius Ponticus
    - _The Sayings of the Desert Fathers_ – Benedicta Ward (trans.)
    - _The Philokalia_ (Vol. 1) – includes Evagrius’ texts
3. **Catholic Contemplative Tradition (Bonaventure)**: A path of mystical theology within Catholicism stressing contemplation, ascent of the soul, and union with God.
    
    - _The Soul’s Journey into God_ – Bonaventure
    - _The Triple Way_ – Bonaventure
    - _Bonaventure: Mystic of God’s Light_ – Ilia Delio
4. **Hesychasm (Gregory Palamas)**: An Eastern Orthodox mystical practice of inner stillness, prayer of the heart, and direct experience of divine light.
    
    - _The Triads_ – Gregory Palamas
    - _Philokalia_ (Vol. 4) – includes Palamas’ writings
    - _Gregory Palamas: The Hesychast Controversy_ – John Meyendorff
5. **Chivalry (Wolfram von Eschenbach)**: A Christianized knightly code of honor infused with mystical ideals of service, purity, and spiritual quest (as in _Parzival_).
    
    - _Parzival_ – Wolfram von Eschenbach
    - _The Grail Legend_ – Emma Jung & Marie-Louise von Franz
    - _The Holy Grail: The History of a Legend_ – Richard Barber
6. **Christian Alchemy (George Ripley)**: The use of alchemical symbolism to represent inner transformation and spiritual regeneration in a Christian framework.
    
    - _The Compound of Alchemy_ – George Ripley
    - _Theatrum Chemicum Britannicum_ – Elias Ashmole (ed.)
    - _Alchemy: The Medieval Alchemists and Their Royal Art_ – Johannes Fabricius
7. **Rhineland Mysticism (Meister Eckhart)**: A medieval German mystical current emphasizing detachment, the inner spark of the soul, and union with God.
    
    - _Meister Eckhart: Selected Writings_ – Oliver Davies (trans.)
    - _Theologia Germanica_ – Anonymous (linked with Eckhart’s circle)
    - _Meister Eckhart: Teacher and Preacher_ – Bernard McGinn
8. **Christian Cabala (Johannes Reuchlin)**: A Renaissance adaptation of Jewish Kabbalah into a Christian theological and mystical system.
    
    - _On the Art of the Kabbalah (De Arte Cabalistica)_ – Johannes Reuchlin
    - _Kabbalah and Philosophy_ – Gershom Scholem
    - _Christian Kabbalah: Jewish Mystical Books and Their Christian Interpreters_ – Joseph Dan
9. **Paracelsianism (Paracelsus)**: A Christian esoteric healing philosophy combining alchemy, astrology, and natural magic with divine inspiration.
    
    - _Selected Writings_ – Paracelsus
    - _Paracelsus: Essential Theoretical Writings_ – Andrew Weeks (ed.)
    - _Paracelsus: Medicine, Magic and Mission at the End of Time_ – Charles Webster
10. **Rosicrucianism (Robert Fludd)**: A mystical brotherhood tradition blending Christianity, Hermeticism, and esoteric sciences, seeking universal reformation.

- _The Rosicrucian Manifestos_ – Frances Yates (intro)
- _The Temple of Music_ – Robert Fludd
- _The Rosicrucian Enlightenment_ – Frances Yates

11. **Christian Theosophy (Jakob Böhme)**: A visionary mystical philosophy centered on divine revelation, inner illumination, and the dynamic interplay of God and creation.

- _The Way to Christ_ – Jakob Böhme
- _Aurora_ – Jakob Böhme
- _Theosophy of Jakob Böhme_ – Alexandre Koyré

12. **Martinism (Louis Claude de Saint-Martin)**: A Christian mystical and initiatic movement stressing inner reintegration with the divine through contemplation and theurgy.

- _Man: His True Nature and Ministry_ – Saint-Martin
- _The Ministry of Man the Spirit-Medium_ – Saint-Martin
- _The Unknown Philosopher_ – Arthur Edward Waite

13. **Swedenborgianism (Emanuel Swedenborg)**: A Christian mystical tradition based on Swedenborg’s visions of the spiritual world and symbolic interpretation of Scripture.

- _Heaven and Hell_ – Emanuel Swedenborg
- _Divine Love and Wisdom_ – Emanuel Swedenborg
- _Swedenborg: Introducing the Mystic_ – Gary Lachman

14. **Magical Idealism (Novalis)**: A Romantic Christian esoteric philosophy blending poetry, mysticism, and transcendental idealism.

- _Hymns to the Night_ – Novalis
- _Novalis: Philosophical Writings_ – Margaret Mahony Stoljar (trans.)
- _The Early Political Writings of Novalis_ – Frederick Beiser

15. **Romanticism (Franz von Baader)**: A Christian mystical approach within German Romanticism, emphasizing divine love, symbolism, and esoteric philosophy.

- _Lectures on Divine Things_ – Franz von Baader
- _Franz von Baader’s Social and Religious Thought_ – Jonathan Auguste
- _German Romanticism and Its Institutions_ – Elizabeth Millán-Zaibert

16. **Anthroposophy (Rudolf Steiner)**: A spiritual science growing from Christian esotericism, integrating mysticism, education, and cosmic evolution.

- _Theosophy: An Introduction to the Supersensible Knowledge of the World_ – Rudolf Steiner
- _Knowledge of the Higher Worlds and Its Attainment_ – Rudolf Steiner
- _Rudolf Steiner: An Introduction to His Life and Work_ – Gary Lachman

17. **Sophiology (Sergei Bulgakov)**: Russian mystical theology centered on Sophia, divine wisdom, as a mediating principle between God and creation.

- _Sophia, the Wisdom of God_ – Sergei Bulgakov
- _The Bride of the Lamb_ – Sergei Bulgakov
- _The Burning Bush: On the Orthodox Veneration of the Mother of God_ – Sergei Bulgakov

18. **Christian Hermeticism (Valentin Tomberg)**: A 20th-century esoteric synthesis of Catholic mysticism, Hermetic philosophy, and Tarot symbolism.

- _Meditations on the Tarot: A Journey into Christian Hermeticism_ – Anonymous (attributed to Tomberg)
- _Covenant of the Heart_ – Valentin Tomberg
- _Christ and Sophia_ – Valentin Tomberg

19. **Fourth Way (Boris Mouravieff)**: A Christian esoteric teaching system emphasizing inner transformation and integration of spiritual practice into daily life.

- _Gnosis: Study and Commentaries on the Esoteric Tradition of Eastern Orthodoxy (Vol. 1–3)_ – Boris Mouravieff
- _The Fourth Way_ – P.D. Ouspensky (related tradition)
- _In Search of the Miraculous_ – P.D. Ouspensky

20. **Christian Traditionalism (Jean Borella)**: A modern esoteric theology rooted in Catholic tradition, defending perennial wisdom and mystical orthodoxy.

- _The Crisis of Religious Symbolism_ – Jean Borella
- _The Sense of the Supernatural_ – Jean Borella
- _The Secret of the Christian Way_ – Jean Borella

21. **Divine Love (James Padgett)**: A mystical Christian revelation emphasizing God’s love as transformative, conveyed through Padgett’s channeled writings.

- _True Gospel Revealed Anew by Jesus_ – James Padgett (channeled writings)
- _The Padgett Messages_ – Compilations of his mediumship
- _The True Gospel Revealed Again_ – Foundation Church of the New Birth edition
