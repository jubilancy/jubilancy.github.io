## Simple Security Measures
- [Secure Wi-FI](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-secure-your-home-wi-fi-network "Using a public home Wi-Fi with a strong password and encryption(such as WPA2 and WPA3) is great for preventing unauthorized access by others")
- [Regular Browser Updates](https://oit.utk.edu/security/learning-library/article-archive/keeping-your-web-browser-updated/#:~:text=Regular%20browser%20updates%20help%20patch,your%20exposure%20to%20security%20flaws. "Keeping your web browser up to date to protect against known vulnerabilities and security threats. Enabling automatic updates ensures that your browser is always protected with the latest security measures.")
- [Strong Passwords](https://www.lastpass.com/features/password-generator "For any account, you'll want a password. Creating a password that is unique is generally a great choice for an account, but if you want more security, make it long, use capitalized letters, numbers, and special characters. You can also avoid using easily guessable information like birthdays or common words.")
- [Secure Browsers](https://www.zdnet.com/article/5-reasons-why-i-use-firefox-when-i-need-the-most-secure-web-browser/ "You should anyways want to use secure browsers that prevents you from going on untrusted sites, such as firefox")
- [Device Lock](https://it.nmu.edu/docs/setting-passcode-your-mobile-device#windows8 "On many devices, there is a required password or PIN that is needed to access the device, such as a phone or laptop.")
- [Ad Blocker](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/ublock-origin/cjpalhdlnbpafiamejdnhcphjbkeiagm "Ad Blockers prevent page elements, such as advertisements, from being displayed, I recommend uBlock Origin.")
You don't exactly want the greatest of security, just some everyday security measures will do.
## Improved Security Features
- [Password Manager](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/top-tips-for-staying-secure-online/password-managers "When you save a password on google or edge, you're using a password manager, this is a software used to secure all of your passwords to all of your accounts. They can also generate strong password for you.")
- [Two-Factor Authentication](https://www.techtarget.com/searchsecurity/definition/two-factor-authentication "2FA, is a extra security layer for an account, that requires another form of identification to access an account, usually in a SMS code, aka a code sent to your email or phone number.")
- [Security Settings](https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query=How+to+manage+security+settings "When using any OS, like windows or MacOS, they usually have security settings you can change, if you want better security, you can tweak your settings to allow the best security on your computer.")
- [Site's Connection](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95617?hl=en "Every site you visit should have a stable connection, if not, try to avoid the website, and don't share any personal information on the website")
- [HTTPS Only Sites](https://https.cio.gov/faq/#what-does-https-do "Visit HTTPS websites and try to avoid HTTP websites, as they less security than HTTPS. You can check in the URL of the website, but be careful, even HTTPS only websites can be dangerous.")
- [Protective Case](https://www.mobileworxs.com/blog/2023/07/11/10-reasons-why-you-need-a-protective-case/ "A case put around your smart device, protecting it from scratches and falls.")
You took the time to install additional features to keep yourself more safe online, Nice!
## Enchanted Security
- [VPN](https://azure.microsoft.com/en-us/resources/cloud-computing-dictionary/what-is-vpn#:~:text=A%20VPN%2C%20which%20stands%20for,and%20firewalls%20on%20the%20internet. "A virtual private network is something that is in between the connection of your computer and the internet. It takes your IP address and changes it to somewhere else in the world, and it also encrypts your personal data that is shared on the internet.")
- [Proxys/Proxychains](https://www.fortinet.com/resources/cyberglossary/proxy-server#:~:text=A%20proxy%20server%20is%20a,watching%20movies%20online%2C%20for%20example. "A Proxy is something in between the connection of your computer and the internet, that simply places your IP address elsewhere in the world. A Proxychain simply does this multiple times, meaning it can be about 3 or 4 Proxys in between your computer and the internet.")
- [Antivirus Software](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/guidance/what-is-an-antivirus-product "An antivirus prevents malware from entering your system by scanning files and programs for known threats and suspicious behavior, if it is detected, then it will block or remove the detected threat.")
- [Ethernet Connection](https://www.wired.com/story/what-is-ethernet/ "Using a ethernet(wired) connection from your router to devices makes it more difficult to access wired connection ensures a stable and secure link, making it more challenging to access the network without permission.")
- [Tor Browser](https://www.torproject.org/download/ "The Tor Browser is used to browse anonymously by connecting to the Tor network. It passes through 3 layers that encrypts your data and hides your IP address each layer.")
- [No Cookies Allowed](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416?hl=en&co=GENIE.Platform%3DDesktop "Having no cookies allowed means that the website that you're visiting can't collect information about you or send that information to other websites, but it makes your life less convenient since you won't have your username or password saved on any of the websites you commonly visit, meaning you always have to type it in yourself.")
Below the waters, you start to concern yourself with your security, nothing crazy though.
## Fortified Security
- [Faraday bags](https://faradaybag.com/what-is-a-faraday-bag/ "When a device is placed inside this bag, all electromagnetic fields, which prevents unauthorized tracking and data theft since nothing can send signals to your device.")
- [Security Key](https://www.hypr.com/security-encyclopedia/security-key "A security key is a physical device used for authentication of an account, if your account asks for your security key, you must plug in your device in your computer in order to gain access.")
- [Cloud Browser](https://www.ringcentral.com/gb/en/blog/definitions/cloud-browser/ "A cloud browser operates as a separate virtual machine on your computer, isolating itself to ensure that any potential threats or issues do not affect your main system")
- [Disaster Recovery Plan](https://www.recordnations.com/blog/it-disaster-recovery-plan/ "If you lose data from your computer, your disaster recovery plan (DRP) saves your critical data and stores it in a secure off-site location. This can be a physical place, such as a data center, or a cloud backup server. If your hard drive were to fail, you can still recover your important data from these secure backups.")
- [Multi-Hop VPN](https://www.netgear.com/hub/network/multi-hop-vpn/#:~:text=Multi%2DHop%20VPNs%20encrypt%20and,successful%20cyber%20attacks%20and%20surveillance. "Multi-hop VPNs are a feature in certain VPNs that route your internet traffic through multiple servers instead of just one, like a normal VPN does. This process encrypts your information multiple times, providing layers of protection against surveillance and tracking. It makes it much harder for anyone to trace your online activity.")
- [Secure DNS](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iV8FYhYrUxI "A DNS is a machine that make a human-readable website/domain name and turns it into a IP address, so that the browser can find the website you're looking for. Go to your security settings, enter your network and internet, go to Wi-Fi or Ethernet, then your Wi-fi or Ethernet properties, to configure the DNS settings. The recommended preferred DNS is 1.1.1.1, and the alternate DNS is 1.0.0.1 for Ivp4, and be sure to enabled DNS over Https to encrypt your personal data. If you want to go a step further, host your own DNS server to connect to so that you don't share your info to the DNS.")
Your internet security is a great priority of yours, and you take great measures to defend it.
