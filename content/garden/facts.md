Shazam doesn’t actually “listen” to songs. It converts sound into a mathematical fingerprint using FFT — turning audio into a map of frequency peaks. Then it matches patterns, not sound. It’s not audio recognition. It’s geometry. That’s why it can identify a song in seconds from just 5 seconds of audio.

Born in 1940 in Edinburgh and raised in Liverpool, Sutcliffe met John Lennon at the Liverpool College of Art. In 1960, he joined Lennon, Paul McCartney, George Harrison, and Pete Best during their early Hamburg days. Though often criticized for his bass playing, Sutcliffe brought style and artistic influence to the group, his “mop-top” haircut would later become part of The Beatles’ iconic look.  
  
In 1961, Sutcliffe left the band to focus on painting, enrolling at the Hamburg College of Art. Just months later, on April 10, 1962, he died in Hamburg at age 21 from a brain hemorrhage.  
  
At the time of his death, The Beatles had not yet released their first single, “Love Me Do,” which debuted later that year. Beatlemania, and international superstardom, was still ahead.  
  
Added fact: Paul McCartney only took over as full-time bassist after Sutcliffe left, shaping the melodic bass style that became central to The Beatles’ sound. source [^1]
