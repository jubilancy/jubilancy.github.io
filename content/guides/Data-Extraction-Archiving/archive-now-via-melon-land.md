The Internet Archive is a wonderful resource and it's great to add your archives to it, but you really should be keeping local archives too. **don't depend on Archive.org!!** Also

  

Some great programs Im aware off that can be used for local archiving are listed here: (Feel free to mention more)

**YouTube and other videos:**

-   [YouTube DL Gui](https://github.com/jely2002/youtube-dl-gui/releases/tag/v2.4.0) - Simple-ish local downloader, though possibly abandoned? (Mac?, Windows, Linux) Free (very easy)
-   [Stacher](https://stacher.io/) - same as above but different! (Mac?, Windows, Linux) Free (very easy)
-   [Downie](https://software.charliemonroe.net/upgrade/downie/v4/) - a simple downloading program (Mac) Paid or with [SetApp](https://setapp.com/apps/downie) (very easy)
-   [YouTube DLP](https://github.com/yt-dlp/yt-dlp) - a command line program (Mac, Windows, Linux) Free (quite techy)

**Websites and Forums:**

-   [SiteSucker](https://ricks-apps.com/osx/sitesucker/index.html) - a great easy to use downloader (Mac, iOS) Paid on modern macs, free on retro ones (very easy)
-   [HTTrack](https://www.httrack.com/) - like SiteSucker for Windows however its abandonware and may be outdated (Windows, Linux) (easy)
-   [ArchiveBox](https://archivebox.io/) - command line or with a UI, this is prob the most up to date Windows option (Mac, Windows, Linux) Free (a bit techy)
-   [wget](https://superuser.com/questions/1415717/how-to-download-an-entire-site-with-wget-including-its-images) - because people will shout if I don't mention it (Mac, Windows, Linux) Free (very techy)

_Updated to a newer working version!_ (quite techy, but not too hard) -[this tool to download sites from the WayBackMachine](https://github.com/ShiftaDeband/wayback-machine-downloader) Use

  

Most arching programs do have a bit of a learning curve to get what you want, but it's worth it! Ideally, you want websites and video archives to be browsable and functional when you check back on them in the future, a broken or obscure archive is not a very good archive - so keep your organization in check too! Archive whole youtube channels if possible, not just one video, and archive whole websites not just one page.
