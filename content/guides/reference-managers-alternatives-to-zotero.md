### TL;DR
*   **[Mendeley Reference Manager](https://www.mendeley.com/)**: Best for social networking and group collaboration on research.
*   **[EndNote](https://endnote.com/)**: The "industry standard" for high-volume academic publishing (paid).
*   **[Citavi](https://www.citavi.com/)**: Includes a unique "Task Planner" and knowledge organizer for complex projects (Windows only).
*   **[Paperpile](https://paperpile.com/)**: A lightweight, Google Chrome-centric manager that syncs directly with Google Drive.
*   **[Juris-M](https://juris-m.github.io/)**: A specialized version of Zotero designed for complex legal and multilingual citations.

### **1. Mendeley Reference Manager**
Owned by Elsevier, [Mendeley](https://www.mendeley.com/) is the most common free alternative for those who find Zotero’s interface outdated.
*   **Strength:** Excellent PDF annotation tools and a built-in "Social" feed to follow other researchers' collections.
*   **Storage:** Offers 2GB of free cloud storage for your collection.
*   **Browser Extension:** Its "Web Importer" is highly effective at detecting metadata on [Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/) and academic journals.

### **2. Citavi**
[Citavi](https://www.citavi.com/) goes beyond simple bookmarking by including a "Knowledge Organization" suite.
*   **Unique Feature:** You can save individual "Knowledge Items" (quotes, thoughts, or image captions) rather than just the whole article link.
*   **Planning:** It has a built-in task manager to help you track which biographies you’ve read and which ones you still need to research.
*   **Limitation:** It is a Windows-based application, though a web version is available.

### **3. Paperpile**
If you prefer a cloud-based workflow without a desktop app, [Paperpile](https://paperpile.com/) is the leading choice.
*   **Integration:** It is built specifically for **Google Chrome** and **Google Docs**.
*   **PDF Management:** It automatically organizes your saved PDFs in a specific folder on your Google Drive.
*   **Cost:** It has a free trial, but generally requires a small monthly subscription for full features.

### **4. JabRef**
For users who prefer an open-source, local-first approach similar to Zotero but with a different logic.
*   **Format:** It uses **BibTeX** as its native format, making it the best choice if you ever plan to use LaTeX for your collection.
*   **Privacy:** It is entirely offline and does not require a cloud account to function.
