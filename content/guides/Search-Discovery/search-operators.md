# Refine Google searches

There are different ways to refine your Google searches to be more precise or to expand in new directions.

## Advanced search

Google offers pages designed to help you perform specialized searches.

To do an advanced search:

1. In the “Google Search” box, enter your query.
2. At the bottom of the search box, click **Tools**.
    - Or, tap **Search Tools** on mobile.
3. Under “Tools,” click **Advanced Search**.

## Filters

After you perform a search, filters appear below the search bar. Filters allow you to narrow results to a particular type, such as:

- Books
- Forums
- Images
- Maps
- News
- Shopping
- Short Videos
- Videos
- Web

**Tips:**

- The "Web" filter contains text-based links to websites.
- The exact filters and the order in which they appear is dynamic. It's based on what our systems might think are most helpful to your query. If you don't find a particular filter that you want, use the "All" option to check others that are available.
- Filters are also available through Advanced Search.

## Operators

To narrow your results in specific ways, you can use special operators in your search. Do not put spaces between the operator and your search term. A search for [`site:nytimes.com`] will work, but [`site: nytimes.com`] won't. Here are some popular operators:

- **To search for an exact match of a word or phrase:** Enter a word or a phrase inside quotes `“”`.
    - For example, [`"tallest building"`]
- **To search for results from a specific site:** Enter `site:` in front of a site or domain.
    - For example, [`site:youtube.com cat videos`]
- **To exclude words from your search:** Enter `-` in front of a word that you want to leave out.
    - For example, [`jaguar speed -car`]
- **To find documents last updated before a particular date:** Enter `before:` in front of a year or specific date.
    - For example, [`astronomy discovery before:2025/11/01`] or [`avengers endgame before:2019`]
- **To find documents last updated after a particular date:** Enter `after:` in front of a year or specific date.
    - For example, [`astronomy discoveries after:2026/03/10`] or [`avengers endgame after:2020`]
- **To find documents last updated within a particular date range:** Combine the `before:` and `after:` operators.
    - For example, [`avengers endgame after:2019-03-01 before:2019-03-05`]
- **To find documents of a particular file type:** Enter `filetype:` in front of the file type.
    - For example, [`photoelectric effect paper filetype:pdf`]

## Related results

After you click a web result and return back to the search results, you may find more related results added to what was originally generated. These added results are related to the web result that you clicked on.

## Related searches

After a search, you find related searches to what you originally searched for. Related searches are automatically generated based on what our systems understand about how people search.

## Settings

In Google Search, you can use settings that allow you to match or remove certain types of content:

- You can filter Google Search results to different languages with the [Language results filter](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/13485060).
- You can filter explicit content from your results with [SafeSearch](https://support.google.com/websearch/answer/510).
