Editor assistance in creating articles

For the [WikiProject](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject "Wikipedia:WikiProject"), see [Wikipedia:WikiProject Articles for creation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Articles_for_creation "Wikipedia:WikiProject Articles for creation").

- [WP:AFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:AFC&redirect=no)[WP:AFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AFC "Wikipedia:AFC")

[![](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/2/21/AFC-Logo.svg/120px-AFC-Logo.svg.png)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/File:AFC-Logo.svg) Welcome to Articles for creation!

The **Articles for creation** ( **AfC**) process is designed to assist _any_ editor with creating a new page as a [draft article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Drafts "Wikipedia:Drafts"), as well as submitting their draft article for review. This process must be used by [new](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:REGISTERED "Wikipedia:REGISTERED") or [unregistered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:UNREGISTERED "Wikipedia:UNREGISTERED") editors, since they cannot directly create a new page in Wikipedia's [mainspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:MAINSPACE "Wikipedia:MAINSPACE"), where all articles reside. This process must also be used by editors with a [conflict of interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest "Wikipedia:Conflict of interest"). Established users are encouraged to create articles on their own if they do not need support from reviewers.

Writing an encyclopedic article from scratch is not easy. It is recommended that you first [gain some experience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Introduction "Help:Introduction") by adding material to pre-existing articles, or by helping out with [other tasks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Task_Center "Wikipedia:Task Center"). You should read [Help:Your first article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Your_first_article "Help:Your first article") to avoid common mistakes. To submit [redirects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Redirect "Wikipedia:Redirect") and [categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Categories "Wikipedia:Categories"), use the [Redirect wizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation/Wizard-Redirects "Wikipedia:Articles for creation/Wizard-Redirects") or [Category wizard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Article_wizard/version1/Category "Wikipedia:Article wizard/version1/Category").

Before you start writing, here are some [things you should have in mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Your_first_article "Help:Your first article"):

- Make sure that the topic is [notable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Notability "Wikipedia:Notability") enough for an article. Is it already covered in an existing article? If not, there is a very good chance that the topic is not notable and will not be accepted as an article. If yes, have you improved that coverage? Improving existing content is a much more successful way of contributing to Wikipedia.
- Articles should reflect only what [reliable sources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:RS "Wikipedia:RS") have said about the topic, and all articles need [references](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Help:Introduction_to_referencing_with_Wiki_Markup/1 "Help:Introduction to referencing with Wiki Markup/1") to reliable sources.
- If you have a [conflict of interest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Conflict_of_interest "Wikipedia:Conflict of interest") with the topic you are writing about, you should [disclose it on the draft's talk page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:DISCLOSE "Wikipedia:DISCLOSE").
- [Articles that are generated entirely by LLMs will be rejected.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Writing_articles_with_large_language_models "Wikipedia:Writing articles with large language models")
- If you are being paid to contribute to Wikipedia, you **must**—under the [Wikimedia Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Foundation "Wikimedia Foundation")'s [terms of use](https://wikimediafoundation.org/wiki/Terms_of_Use#4._Refraining_from_Certain_Activities "foundationsite:wiki/Terms of Use")— [disclose who is paying you, who the client is, and any other relevant affiliation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:COIPAYDISCLOSE "Wikipedia:COIPAYDISCLOSE").
- Please do not add [categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Categories "Wikipedia:Categories") to your drafts; they are reserved for finished articles. A reviewer will add categories upon acceptance.
- If you have an article idea but do not want to write it yourself, add it to [Wikipedia:Requested articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Requested_articles "Wikipedia:Requested articles").

Your article title will have " [Draft:](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Drafts "Wikipedia:Drafts")" in front of it until it is accepted.

### Submitting for review

[Shortcut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Shortcut "Wikipedia:Shortcut")

- [WP:AFCREVIEW](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:AFCREVIEW&redirect=no)[WP:AFCREVIEW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:AFCREVIEW "Wikipedia:AFCREVIEW")

Submit your draft for review by clicking the button "Submit for review". If you don't see this button, you can use the AFC submission wizard at [Wikipedia:Articles for creation/Submitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation/Submitting "Wikipedia:Articles for creation/Submitting") (remember to switch to [desktop mode](https://en.wikipedia.org/?title=Wikipedia:Articles_for_creation/Submitting&mobileaction=toggle_view_desktop)) or add the code `{{subst:submit}}` to the top of your draft (remember to switch to the [source editor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Source_editor "Wikipedia:Source editor") first). If you see a yellow box saying "Review waiting, please be patient" on your draft, you have successfully submitted it for review. If you don't see this box, you can ask for help at the [Articles for Creation help desk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:WikiProject_Articles_for_creation/Help_desk "Wikipedia:WikiProject Articles for creation/Help desk").

Getting a review can take a while, but your draft will be reviewed eventually. Attempting to bypass the process by moving the page, or cutting and pasting it into a new mainspace article, may lead to the page being moved back into draftspace again, [speedily-deleted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:CSD "Wikipedia:CSD") or listed for [Articles for Deletion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Articles_for_Deletion "Wikipedia:Articles for Deletion"), and repeated attempts may lead to consequences. In the meantime, we hope that you expand some of our already existing articles.

Reviewers may _decline_ or _reject_ your draft submission. If your draft has been _declined_, you are encouraged to take the reviewer's feedback into account and resubmit your draft using the "submit" button on the AfC template. If your draft has been _rejected_, you are not encouraged to resubmit and will not see a "submit" button on the template. Reviewers _decline_ drafts if they do not presently meet Wikipedia's guidelines. Reviewers _reject_ drafts only if it is unlikely that they will ever meet those guidelines; for example, if the topic is not relevant for the encyclopedia, or if the draft is spam. A topic that is not [notable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:NOTABLE "Wikipedia:NOTABLE"), however, [may later become notable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Before_they_were_notable "Wikipedia:Before they were notable").

## Old drafts

Drafts that have not been edited for more than six months are routinely deleted (see [WP:CSD#G13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:CSD#G13 "Wikipedia:CSD")). Once deleted, they can be recovered upon request at [WP:REFUND/G13](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:REFUND/G13 "Wikipedia:REFUND/G13"). The drafts that are approaching G13 can be found at [Category:AfC G13 eligible soon submissions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:AfC_G13_eligible_soon_submissions "Category:AfC G13 eligible soon submissions"), with the oldest drafts at the top of the category. These can be reviewed and nominated for deletion or improved for mainspace.

Some old drafts have been identified as "promising", but without any active editor actually committing to working on them. See [Category:Promising draft articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category:Promising_draft_articles "Category:Promising draft articles"). Consider browsing these drafts for topics that you would like to improve and move to mainspace. If it appears that {{ [Promising draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template:Promising_draft "Template:Promising draft")}} are not merited, you may remove them but consider first discussing the matter with the original placer of the template or category.
