The following list is meant to help you with your own research, by offering links to **respectable information sources on the web, available free of charge**. Inclusion on the list doesn't automatically mean the absolute truth is on these websites, so always be critical and compare information between different sources.

The content of the subsections is alphabetically organized.

Please add free online sources if you know some that are missing in this list, but try to keep it relevant and trustworthy.

## General resources and link lists

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:List_of_free_online_resources&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section: General resources and link lists")\]

See also: [Wikipedia:Wikimedia sister projects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Wikimedia_sister_projects "Wikipedia:Wikimedia sister projects")

- [Wikiversity](https://en.wikiversity.org/wiki/ "v:") \- sister project that provides information on most academic subjects
- [Google Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Scholar "Google Scholar") [\[1\]](http://www.scholar.google.com/) \- provides a simple way to broadly search for scholarly literature
- [Paperity](http://paperity.org/) \- multidisciplinary aggregator of Open Access journals and papers; provides free full text, advanced search and permanent URLs for all articles
- [ipl2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Public_Library "Internet Public Library") [\[2\]](http://www.ipl.org/) \- merger of the collections of resources from the Internet Public Library (IPL) and the Librarians' Internet Index (LII) websites, hosted by [Drexel University College of Information Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drexel_University_College_of_Information_Science_and_Technology "Drexel University College of Information Science and Technology")
- [Refdesk](http://www.refdesk.com/facts.html) \- free and family-friendly web site that indexes and reviews quality, credible, and current web-based resources
- [DeepDyve](http://www.deepdyve.com/) \- big archive of literary and scholarly journal articles; free five-minute full-text previews.

### Searching for photographs by content

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:List_of_free_online_resources&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section: Searching for photographs by content")\]

See also: [Wikipedia:Public domain image resources](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Public_domain_image_resources "Wikipedia:Public domain image resources")

Resources for sourcing and searching for photographs by the content that is depicted. Though the search engines may be accessed for free, indexed images themselves may be under restricted license.

- [Google Books](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_Books "Google Books") [\[3\]](http://www.books.google.com/) \- Searchable archive of magazines and books (some full-text, including photograph captions and references to photographs from related articles and content).
- United States [Library of Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_of_Congress "Library of Congress") [\[4\]](http://www.loc.gov/) \- Searchable archive of historic photographs, maps, performing arts, newspapers.
- [Life (magazine)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_(magazine) "Life (magazine)") Photo Gallery Archives [(web site)](http://www.life.com/archive/gallery) \- Searchable archive of photographs of news and sporting events, celebrities, travel.
- [NASA Images](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA_Images "NASA Images") [\[5\]](https://www.nasa.gov/image-of-the-day/) \- Searchable archive of [aeronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautics "Aeronautics"), [space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space "Space") and [space exploration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_exploration "Space exploration") photographs.
- Pexels [\[6\]](http://www.pexels.com/) \- Public domain photographs
- LibreShot [(web site)](http://libreshot.com/) \- CC0 1.0 (public domain) photographs

## Biographies

\[ [edit](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Wikipedia:List_of_free_online_resources&action=edit&section=4 "Edit section: Biographies")\]

- _[Australian Dictionary of Biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Dictionary_of_Biography "Australian Dictionary of Biography")_ [\[27\]](http://www.adb.online.anu.edu.au/) \- over 10,000 scholarly biographies of persons who were significant in Australian history
- _[Dictionary of Canadian Biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dictionary_of_Canadian_Biography "Dictionary of Canadian Biography")_ [\[28\]](http://www.biographi.ca/) \- biographies devoted to people who have played an significant role in what is now Canada, who died between the years 1000 and 1930 or whose last known date of activity falls within these years
- _[Oxford Dictionary of National Biography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_Dictionary_of_National_Biography "Oxford Dictionary of National Biography")_ [\[29\]](http://www.oxforddnb.com/) \- collection of more than 57,000 specially written biographies, which describe the lives of people who shaped the history of the British Isles and beyond from the 4th century BC to the 21st century. Login is required to access, and it's not strictly free - however, if you're in the UK, entering your library card number will almost certainly get you in. Many libraries elsewhere in the world also have unrestricted access.
- [Project Gutenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Gutenberg "Project Gutenberg")'s very large collection of free, public-domain [Biographies](http://www.gutenberg.org/wiki/Biographies_(Bookshelf))
